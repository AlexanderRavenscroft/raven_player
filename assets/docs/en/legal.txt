Raven Player Privacy Policy and Terms of Use
Effective Date: 28 June 2026

This Privacy Policy and Terms of Use explains how Raven Player ("App") handles information and sets basic rules for using the App.

1. Introduction
Raven Player is an offline audiobook player for local audio files. The App does not require an account and does not provide, sell, download, stream, or recommend audiobooks.

2. Information We Access
Raven Player accesses only the files and folders you choose or allow through your device or operating system. To display and play your library, the App may read file names, folder references, audio metadata, playback duration, embedded cover images, and file locations.

3. Information We Store
Raven Player stores app data locally on your device. This may include selected folder references, library information, audiobook metadata, extracted cover images, app settings, reading status, and playback progress.

4. How We Use Information
Raven Player uses this information only to provide offline audiobook playback, library organization, settings, and saved progress. The App does not intentionally upload, sell, or share your audiobook files, library contents, playback history, or personal data with us or third parties.

5. Third-Party Services
Raven Player does not use third-party analytics, advertising, telemetry, cloud sync, remote accounts, or external databases. The App may use third-party software libraries and operating system services for local storage, audio playback, background media controls, file access, and app functionality.

6. Data Security, Retention, and Deletion
Raven Player data is stored locally on your device. You can remove local App data using your operating system's app data controls or by uninstalling the App. Removing App data may delete saved settings, library records, extracted cover images, and playback progress. It should not delete your original audiobook files unless your operating system or file manager does so separately.

7. Lawful Use
You are responsible for the audio files you use with Raven Player. You should only use files that you own, are licensed to use, or are otherwise legally allowed to access. You may not use the App to infringe copyright or violate applicable laws.

8. No Warranty
Raven Player is provided "as is". We do not guarantee that the App will be uninterrupted, error-free, compatible with every device, preserve all data, support every audio file, or meet every user need.

9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy and Terms of Use when the App changes or when legal, platform, or operational requirements change. Updates will be reflected by changing the effective date.
